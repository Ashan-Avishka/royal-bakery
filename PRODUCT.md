# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Royal Bakery serves mobile-first Sri Lankan shoppers who want to discover baked
goods, order confidently, and manage carts, checkout, and order history without
friction. It also serves bakery staff and administrators who manage catalog,
inventory, customers, orders, and reports from both phones and desktop systems.

## Product Purpose

Royal Bakery is an integrated bakery storefront and operations system. The
customer experience should make real products easy to browse and purchase; the
administration experience should make daily bakery work clear and efficient.
Success means trustworthy product presentation, fast mobile interaction, calm
commerce flows, and reliable operational workflows across every supported screen.

## Brand Personality

Warm, tactile, and trustworthy. The voice is welcoming and product-led, with
quiet confidence rather than luxury distance or sales pressure.

## Anti-references

- Sparse luxury-editorial layouts that make shopping secondary to atmosphere
- Dense utility storefronts that overwhelm customers with controls and metadata
- Generic cream-and-card AI templates without a specific Royal Bakery identity
- Decorative effects, invented claims, or unsupported freshness and delivery promises
- Desktop interfaces merely scaled down for mobile instead of being recomposed for touch

## Design Principles

1. Let real bakery products and photography carry the experience.
2. Put the user's current task first: discovery and purchase for customers,
   scanning and action for staff.
3. Preserve warmth without sacrificing legibility, speed, or clear hierarchy.
4. Adapt composition and interaction to each device while keeping one consistent
   information architecture.
5. Make every state trustworthy: loading, empty, unavailable, validation, and
   failure states must remain explicit and actionable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, meaningful labels
and alternative text, non-color status cues, reduced motion, 44-by-44-pixel touch
targets where practical, readable mobile form controls, and layouts that remain
usable without horizontal page overflow from 320 pixels upward.
